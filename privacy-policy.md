**Last updated:** November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data the Discord bots operated by **[ Miku ]** ("we", "us", "our") collect, how it is used, and your choices. It applies to: **[Cat Senpai !!]** (each a "Bot", together the "Bots").

By using a Bot, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please remove the Bot from your server and stop using it.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to operate the Bots' features. Depending on the Bot and features you use, this may include:

- **Discord identifiers:** user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, and message IDs.
- **Public profile details:** usernames, display names, and avatars, as provided by Discord.
- **Server configuration and settings:** prefixes, enabled features, log channels, and similar options set by server administrators.
- **Command data:** commands you run and the options you provide, used to carry out the command.
- **Message content:** only where a feature requires it (for example, prefix commands, auto-moderation, or logging). Message content is processed to perform the feature, and we do not read or store messages for any other purpose.
- **Feature-specific data:** such as moderation records (warnings, bans), levels or economy data, or custom content you create.
- **Technical and diagnostic data:** error logs and usage statistics that help us fix bugs and keep the Bots running.

We do **not** knowingly collect passwords, payment information, email addresses, or other sensitive personal information through the Bots.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information to:

- Provide and operate the Bots' features and commands.
- Save server settings and user data you or your server asked the Bot to keep.
- Prevent abuse, enforce our Terms, and keep the Bots secure.
- Diagnose problems and improve performance and reliability.

## 3. Sharing of Information

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your data. We only share data:

- With service providers that host or support the Bots (e.g. hosting and database providers), under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- When required by law, legal process, or a valid government request.
- To protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, or the Bots.

Data displayed by a Bot inside Discord (for example, a leaderboard) is visible to other members of that server.

## 4. Data Retention

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the features, unless a longer period is required by law.

- **Server data** is deleted or anonymized within **[30]** days after a Bot is removed from a server.
- **Logs** are kept for up to **[30]** days.
- User-specific data is kept until you request deletion or the feature no longer needs it.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data, including access controls and secure hosting. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
